SAFETY

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 16 September 2026

SipMate is an adults-only social app. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, trafficking or any other content or conduct that sexualizes, exploits or endangers children.

1. Adults-only service

SipMate is intended for users aged 18 and over. Accounts or content that indicate a user is under 18 may be restricted or removed.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

The following is strictly prohibited on SipMate:

  • Any sexual content involving a person under 18, including CSAM.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation, sextortion or attempts to obtain sexual content from a minor.
  • Content that promotes, facilitates or normalizes the sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Sharing, requesting, trading or directing users to CSAM or other exploitative material.
  • Any attempt to use SipMate to arrange or facilitate abuse, trafficking or exploitation of a child.

3. Reporting and blocking

SipMate provides in-app tools that allow users to report concerning profiles or behavior and to block other users. Reports may be reviewed for violations of our safety standards and other applicable rules.

4. Enforcement

When we identify or receive credible reports of CSAE, CSAM or related child-safety violations, we may remove content, restrict or terminate accounts, preserve relevant information where appropriate, and take other protective actions consistent with applicable law.

5. Reporting to authorities

SipMate complies with applicable child-protection laws and will report confirmed illegal child sexual abuse or exploitation content to the relevant competent authorities or designated reporting bodies when required by law.

6. Cooperation and preservation

Where legally required, SipMate may preserve relevant account or moderation records and cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities investigating child sexual abuse or exploitation.

7. Contact for child-safety matters

For urgent child-safety concerns related to SipMate, contact sipmate.app@gmail.com. Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant account, profile or incident. Do not email or attach illegal sexual content involving minors.

8. Policy updates

We may update these standards as SipMate features, legal requirements and safety practices evolve. The current version will remain publicly available at this URL.
