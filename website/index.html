FAQ

Before you say CHEERS.

Is SipMate a dating app?

No. SipMate is built for drinks, coffee and spontaneous social hangouts — not dating.

Can people see my exact location?

No. Your precise location is not publicly shown. Location is used for nearby discovery and distance.

How does CHEERS work?

Send 🍻 to someone. If they send 🍻 back, it becomes CHEERS and chat unlocks.

What does Premium cost?

1,99 € monthly. Founders: 14,99 € for the first year for the first 100 yearly members, then 17,99 € Early Access, then 19,99 € standard yearly.

Can I hide from Nearby?

Yes. Switch yourself inactive whenever you want to stop appearing to nearby users.
