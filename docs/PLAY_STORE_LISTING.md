## App name

## English (default)

### Short description
Meet nearby people for a drink, coffee or spontaneous hangout.

### Full description
SipMate is a social app for finding people nearby who are up for a drink, coffee or spontaneous hangout — without turning the experience into dating.

See who is active, check what they are currently up for and send a Cheers 🍻. If both people send Cheers, it becomes CHEERS! and chat opens so you can make plans.

With SipMate you can:
- Discover nearby people
- Switch your availability between ACTIVE and INACTIVE
- Show what you are currently up for
- Send Cheers 🍻
- Unlock chat after a mutual Cheers
- Use real-time messaging and read status
- Block and report users when needed
- Use additional Premium discovery and profile features
- Use the app in English, German or Croatian

SipMate is designed for adults. Meet responsibly, choose public places for first meetings and never drink and drive.

## Store positioning notes

- Core mechanic is Cheers 🍻 and mutual CHEERS!, not “match”.
- Target audience: adults only.
- The app contains user-generated profiles, profile photos and chat.
