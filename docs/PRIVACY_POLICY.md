# SipMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

SipMate is a social app that helps adults discover nearby users who are currently interested in a drink, coffee, or casual meetup.

## Information we collect

SipMate may collect account information such as your email address, profile information such as your name, age, city, bio, profile photos and current activity preference, approximate or precise location when you grant location permission, and app activity needed to provide features such as Cheers, chats, blocking and reports.

## How we use information

We use this information to create and maintain your account, show nearby users, calculate distance, provide Cheers and chat features, support Premium features, prevent abuse, process reports and blocks, and improve reliability and safety.

## Location

Location access is used to provide nearby discovery and distance-based features. SipMate requests location permission from the operating system. You can deny or revoke this permission in your device settings, although nearby features may then be limited.

## Payments

Premium purchases are processed through Stripe. SipMate does not store your full payment-card details. Payment processing is subject to Stripe's own privacy and security practices.

## Data storage

SipMate uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage and realtime features. Profile photos and related user data may be stored in Supabase infrastructure.

## Sharing

SipMate does not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the app, process payments, provide hosting, authentication, storage, security and related functionality.

## Safety

Users can block and report other users. Reports may be reviewed to investigate misuse and help protect the community.

## Data retention and deletion

Account data is retained while it is needed to operate the account and provide the service, subject to legal and operational requirements. SipMate includes an in-app account deletion option. Deleting an account is intended to permanently remove the authentication account and user-owned data that is configured to be deleted with it.

Before public Play Store publication, SipMate will also provide a public web page or contact method for account-deletion requests outside the app.

## Children

SipMate is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. Registration enforces a minimum age of 18. The Play Store audience settings should also be configured for adults.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when SipMate features or service providers change.

## Contact

A public support/privacy email address must be added here before production publication.
