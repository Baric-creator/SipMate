# SipMate Community Guidelines

SipMate is for adults aged 18 and older who want to meet people nearby for a drink, coffee or casual social meetup.

## Be respectful
Treat other users with respect. Harassment, threats, bullying, hate speech, discriminatory abuse or repeated unwanted contact are not allowed.

## No sexual exploitation or abusive content
Do not use SipMate to share or request illegal sexual content, exploitative content, non-consensual intimate content or content involving minors.

## No violence or dangerous activity
Do not threaten violence, encourage serious harm, promote dangerous criminal activity or use SipMate to arrange illegal transactions.

## No scams or impersonation
Do not impersonate another person, deceive users about your identity, solicit money through fraud, distribute malware or use SipMate for spam.

## Respect boundaries
A Cheers, message or meetup invitation is not consent to anything else. If someone says no, stops responding or blocks you, respect that decision.

## Meet safely
For first meetings, consider a public place, tell someone you trust where you are going, keep control of your own transportation and drink responsibly.

## Reporting and blocking
SipMate provides tools to report and block users. Blocking removes the blocked person from relevant discovery and communication flows. Reports may be reviewed for safety and moderation.

## Enforcement
SipMate may restrict or remove accounts that violate these guidelines or create a serious safety risk. Severe or illegal conduct may be reported to appropriate authorities where required by law.

These guidelines may be updated as SipMate evolves.
