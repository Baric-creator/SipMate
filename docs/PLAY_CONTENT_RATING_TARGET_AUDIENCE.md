# SipMate — Google Play Content Rating & Target Audience Guide

Last reviewed: 12 September 2026

This file is a practical entry guide for Play Console. Answer the live Play/IARC questionnaire truthfully based on the shipped app. Do not try to force a specific age rating; Google/IARC calculate the rating from the answers.

## Target audience

Recommended selection for SipMate:

- Target age group: **18 and over only**
- App designed for children: **No**
- Children included in target audience: **No**
- If Play Console offers **Restrict access for minors**, enable it for the adult-only release.

Reason: SipMate is an adult social-discovery service centered on meeting people for drinks and includes user profiles, location-based discovery and private chat.

## Core facts to keep consistent everywhere

- SipMate is a **social networking / social discovery** app.
- It is **not a dating app** by product positioning.
- Registration is intended for **18+ users**.
- Users can create profile text and upload profile photos.
- Users can communicate via 1:1 chat after mutual Cheers.
- Users can report and block other users.
- The app references alcoholic drinks as part of the social concept.
- SipMate does **not sell, deliver or facilitate the purchase of alcohol**.
- There is no gambling, no real-money wagering and no simulated casino mechanic.
- There is no pornography or developer-provided sexual content.
- There is no developer-provided violence, gore or drug-use content.
- User-generated content can vary, so moderation/report/block controls are important.

## IARC / Content Rating questionnaire — recommended factual answers

The exact wording can vary by country and Play Console version. Use these facts when the matching questions appear.

### App type / communication

- Does the app allow users to interact or communicate with each other? **Yes**
- Can users exchange free-form text? **Yes**
- Can users upload or share user-generated content? **Yes**
- Does the app contain user profiles? **Yes**
- Does the app allow private/direct communication? **Yes**
- Does the app expose users to content created by other users? **Yes**
- Are report/block or moderation tools available? **Yes**

### Alcohol

- Does the app contain references to alcohol? **Yes**
- Does it show or discuss alcoholic drinks as part of its theme? **Yes**
- Does it sell alcohol? **No**
- Does it facilitate alcohol purchases or delivery? **No**
- Does it encourage dangerous alcohol consumption? **No**

Do not answer “No alcohol” merely because SipMate does not sell alcohol. Beer, cocktails and meeting for a drink are explicit parts of the product concept.

### Sexual / dating content

- Is the app primarily a dating service? **No**
- Developer-provided sexual content or nudity: **No**
- Explicit sexual content: **No**

If a questionnaire asks whether users could potentially exchange mature content through UGC/chat, answer according to the wording and the actual moderation setup. Do not claim user content is pre-screened if it is not.

### Violence / drugs / gambling

For the current SipMate product:

- Developer-provided violence/gore: **No**
- Developer-provided illegal-drug use: **No**
- Gambling / simulated gambling: **No**
- Real-money gambling: **No**

Again, if a question is about what users might post rather than what SipMate itself provides, follow the wording literally.

### Location / real-world interaction

If asked about location or real-world meetups:

- Location-based discovery: **Yes**
- Users may meet in the real world: **Yes**
- Precise coordinates are not intentionally displayed publicly to other users.

## Advertising

Current recommendation:

- Contains ads: **No**

Change this before release if advertising is added.

## Store listing consistency

Keep these statements aligned across Store Listing, Privacy Policy, Data Safety and Content Rating:

- Adults only / 18+
- Social discovery, not dating
- Nearby discovery uses location
- User-generated profiles/photos/chat exist
- Alcohol is referenced as a social context, not sold by SipMate
- Report and block features exist

## What not to do

- Do not select child age groups to increase reach.
- Do not hide the alcohol references.
- Do not describe SipMate as having fully pre-moderated UGC unless every post/photo/message is actually reviewed before publication.
- Do not select “No user interaction” because chat requires mutual Cheers.
- Do not select “No location” because Nearby relies on location.
- Do not guess an age rating manually; complete the questionnaire and let IARC calculate it.

## Expected result

Because SipMate is adult-only by design and includes social interaction, UGC, location-based discovery and alcohol references, the resulting regional rating can be higher than a generic social app. That is acceptable. Accuracy is more important than obtaining the lowest possible rating.

In Germany, Google Play uses the USK system. Other countries may display different IARC-equivalent labels even from the same questionnaire answers.

## Final Play Console sequence

1. Open **Policy and programs / App content** (wording may vary).
2. Complete **Target audience and content** first.
3. Select **18 and over only**.
4. Enable minor restriction if offered for the adult-only app.
5. Open **Content ratings** and start the IARC questionnaire.
6. Enter the developer contact email requested by IARC.
7. Answer using the factual guidance above.
8. Submit the questionnaire and save the generated ratings.
9. Reopen the result and confirm the answers mention social/user interaction and alcohol where applicable.
10. Revisit this form whenever app content or communication features materially change.
